# Feature 1: SKU Creation and Item Identification

## Short Description

SKU (Stock Keeping Unit) Management is the process of assigning a unique
internal code to each product or product variant so that items can be
identified and tracked accurately in a store or inventory system.

A SKU may contain letters, numbers, or both. It can represent useful
product attributes such as category, brand, size, colour, or model. Each
distinct product variant should have its own unique SKU.

## Purpose

The main purpose of SKU management is to make it easy to identify the
exact item being stored, sold, received, or counted.

It helps a store to:

-   Track inventory accurately.
-   Distinguish similar products and product variants.
-   Locate and retrieve items more easily.
-   Improve the accuracy of sales and inventory records.
-   Support stock counting and replenishment.
-   Reduce errors caused by duplicate or unclear item identification.

## Main Functions / Activities

1.  **Create SKU codes** -- Assign a unique code to each product or
    product variant.
2.  **Maintain item information** -- Store details associated with each
    SKU, such as product name, category, size, colour, unit of measure,
    and description.
3.  **Check SKU uniqueness** -- Prevent two different products or
    variants from being assigned the same SKU.
4.  **Search and identify items** -- Allow store staff to find an item
    using its SKU.
5.  **Update SKU records** -- Modify item information when necessary
    while maintaining accurate inventory records.
6.  **Link SKUs to stock records** -- Connect each SKU with quantities
    held in the store or warehouse.
7.  **Support barcode identification** -- Where applicable, associate a
    SKU with a barcode used for scanning and faster identification.
8.  **Track product variants** -- Give different variants, such as
    different sizes or colours, separate identifiers.

## Research Basis

SKUs are internal identifiers created by a business for inventory
tracking. Shopify's documentation notes that unique SKUs help with
inventory tracking, reporting, and fulfillment, and recommends keeping
SKU structures simple and consistent.
