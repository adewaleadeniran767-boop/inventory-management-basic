# Feature 2: Stock Receiving and Bin/Location Management

## Short Description

Stock Receiving and Bin/Location Management is the process of recording
incoming goods and assigning them to specific storage areas, shelves,
bins, or other locations within a store or warehouse.

When stock arrives, the store can verify what was received, record the
quantities, and move the items from the receiving area to an appropriate
storage location. The system can then keep track of how much stock is
stored at each location.

## Purpose

The main purpose of this feature is to keep physical stock organized and
ensure that the inventory system accurately reflects where items are
stored.

It helps a store to:

-   Record incoming inventory accurately.
-   Know where each item is stored.
-   Make items easier to find and retrieve.
-   Maintain accurate quantities by storage location.
-   Support efficient picking and restocking.
-   Reduce misplaced or unaccounted-for stock.

## Main Functions / Activities

1.  **Receive incoming stock** -- Record products and quantities
    arriving from suppliers or other sources.
2.  **Verify received items** -- Compare received goods with the
    expected items, such as those listed on a purchase order.
3.  **Assign storage locations** -- Decide which shelf, bin, aisle, bay,
    or other location should hold the received stock.
4.  **Record putaway** -- Record the movement of goods from the
    receiving area to their storage locations.
5.  **Track stock by location** -- Keep a record of the quantity of each
    item stored at each location.
6.  **Locate items** -- Allow staff to determine where a particular SKU
    is stored.
7.  **Update inventory quantities** -- Increase or adjust stock records
    when items are received and stored.
8.  **Support picking and replenishment** -- Provide the location
    information needed when staff need to retrieve items or refill store
    shelves.
9.  **Monitor storage capacity** -- Where supported, consider available
    space and location limits when assigning stock.

## Research Basis

Oracle's warehouse-management documentation describes bin management as
a way to identify storage places and track on-hand quantities within
them. Oracle also describes putaway as moving received inventory from a
receiving area to a storage location and updating inventory records.
